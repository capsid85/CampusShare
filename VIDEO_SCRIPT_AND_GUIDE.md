## Word-for-Word Script

### [0:00 - 1:30] Member 1: Introduction & Empathy Phase
*(Show Slide 1, then transition through Slides 2, 3, 4, 5)*

> **"A very warm welcome to Dr. Vandana Jha ma'am and everyone evaluating our Design Thinking project. Today, our team is presenting our project: **CampusShare**, a peer-to-peer campus resource and book exchange designed specifically for engineering students at MIT Bengaluru.**
>
> **Every semester, freshers and students are required to buy specialized supplies—such as B.S. Grewal's Higher Engineering Mathematics, Casio fx-991 scientific calculators, chemistry lab coats, and mini drafters. Students incur heavy recurring expenses purchasing brand-new supplies each term. The tragedy is that once end-semester examinations conclude, these costly tools sit completely idle and neglected in hostel cupboards.**
>
> **During our Empathy phase, we conducted qualitative observational field research directly on active MIT Bengaluru student exchange channels. We discovered students constantly trying to sell accidental double online orders like MacBook covers, midnight food surplus to prevent waste, sealed pantry food, gym supplements, and even splitting digital study subscriptions! Unstructured WhatsApp groups completely fail for this: notices get buried within minutes under relentless banter, people play frustrating 'DM for price' games, and there is zero security. What students truly needed was a verified, hyper-local circular platform."**

---

### [1:30 - 3:00] Member 2: Problem Statement & Analyze Phase
*(Show Slides 6, 7, 8)*

> **"Thank you, [Member 1]. Moving to the Define and Analyze phases.**
>
> **Based on our user research, our official problem statement is:**  
> *'Undergraduate engineering students at MIT Bengaluru incur substantial recurring financial burdens each semester purchasing temporary academic resources that are abandoned after single-term use, because informal campus communication channels lack verification, itemized search, and structured peer-to-peer lending models—leaving senior student inventory unutilized.'*
>
> **To get to the root cause, we used the 5-Whys technique:**
> 1. *Why do freshers overspend on tools?* Because they buy brand new copies from outside retail shops.
> 2. *Why don't they get them from seniors?* Because seniors leave them locked in hostel rooms.
> 3. *Why don't seniors sell or lend them?* Because posting on social groups is unorganized and tedious.
> 4. *Why are social groups ineffective?* Because chat apps lack categories, pricing tags, and search filters.
> 5. *Root Cause:* There is no dedicated, campus-exclusive web portal tailored for student lending and physical handovers.
>
> **This led us to our Solve Phase where we designed a unique multi-tier exchange—including a special feature for emergency, one-time needs:**
> - **🚨 Express Exam & Lab SOS:** For acute 1-day or 3-hour needs—like when a student forgets their calculator 30 minutes before an end-sem exam or needs a lab coat for an unexpected viva. They can rent it for just ₹20 right outside the exam hall or lab block.
> - **Borrow for Free:** For semester-long textbooks.
> - **Rent at low cost:** For 2-week exam blocks.
> - **Buy at 75% discount:** For permanent handover from graduating seniors.
>
> **Now, I will hand over to [Member 3] for a live demonstration of our working prototype."**

### [3:00 - 5:00] Member 3: Live Prototype Demonstration
*(Switch screen share to the browser showing `prototype/index.html`)*

> **"Thank you, [Member 2]. As you can see on the screen, this is our live working prototype for CampusShare.**
>
> *(Click and point to the header & stats)*  
> **Notice at the top that the platform is restricted to verified `@learner.manipal.edu` student credentials. In the hero section, we display our core design pillars: Zero Academic Waste, 100% Verified student accounts, and Zero Platform Intermediary Fees.**
>
> *(Click on '🚨 Exam & Lab SOS (1-Day / Single Use)' filter button)*  
> **Here is our most popular feature: 'Exam & Lab SOS'. Most students don't need a calculator all year; they panic on the morning of a math exam when their battery dies, or when they forget their lab apron back in the hostel room. Instead of being forced to buy a new ₹1,500 calculator or miss their lab viva, a student can click '🚨 Instant SOS Claim' for a ₹20 slot and pick it up right at the Exam Hall Gate or outside the Science Lab in under 5 minutes.**
> *(Click on '⚡ Flash Drops (Food & Hostel Surplus)' filter button)*  
> **"Furthermore, we solved the frustrating 'DM for price' spam and food waste on campus. We took inspiration directly from real WhatsApp group posts: students listing 3:25 AM egg parathas to prevent food waste, surplus sealed Yoga Bar muesli, accidental duplicate MacBook cases from Amazon, or fitness supplements. In CampusShare, every listing has 100% upfront fixed pricing, countdown timers on hot food, and zero hidden price games."**
>
> *(Click on '🎟️ Subscriptions & Digital Splits' filter button)*  
> **"Notice our Digital Subscriptions tab: verified students can list private profile slots on their personal Netflix or Spotify Family plans for ₹99 or ₹40 a month. Campus peers can split digital subscriptions safely through verified `@learner.manipal.edu` identities, completely preventing credential theft or advance payment defaults."**
>
> *(Click on 'Borrow (Free)' filter button)*  
> **For full-semester needs, a fresher can filter only items available for Free Semester Borrowing. For example, here is Higher Engineering Mathematics by B.S. Grewal listed by a Senior Peer (3rd Year CSE), with pickup right at the Central Library Ground Floor.**
>
> *(Click on 'Rent (Low Cost)' filter button)*  
> **If a student only needs a Casio fx-991CW calculator for the 2 weeks of university exams, they can rent it for just ₹40 a week instead of buying a ₹1,500 new piece.**
>
> *(Point cursor to card specs: 'Handover Spot' and 'Privacy Protocol')*  
> **Notice our Real-World Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture: On the public feed, we NEVER display student phone numbers or hostel room numbers—only public campus landmarks like the Central Library or Food Court. This completely eliminates spam and protects student safety, especially for female students in hostel blocks.**
>
> *(Click 'Chat & Meet at Spot' on an item to open the slide-over chat drawer)*  
> **When a student clicks 'Chat & Meet at Spot', an Apple-style slide-over chat drawer opens. Students can negotiate meeting times directly with verified peers using quick suggestion chips—without ever leaking their phone numbers.**
>
> *(Click '📍 Ping "I've Arrived at Spot"' button in the drawer)*  
> **When arriving at the landmark, the buyer clicks 'Ping Arrival Beacon' with a visual cue like 'wearing a black hoodie'. The seller gets an instant ping and steps out.**
>
> *(Click '⚡ On-Spot UPI QR' in the drawer)*  
> **Most importantly, there is NO platform escrow holding student pocket money. The buyer inspects the textbook or calculator physically first. Once satisfied, the seller displays their direct UPI QR code right inside the app. The buyer scans it with GPay or PhonePe for instant bank-to-bank settlement, and both confirm handover.**
>
> *(Click on '+ List an Item' button in navbar)*  
> **Seniors can also click 'List an Item' to post an unused item in under 30 seconds with automatic Zero-Knowledge Ghost Privacy enabled by default.**
>
> **Now, [Member 4] will walk you through our testing results and future scope."**

### [5:00 - 6:30] Member 4: Testing Results & Conclusion
*(Switch screen share back to Slide 13, 14, 15)*

> **"Thank you, [Member 3]. To validate our solution, we conducted interactive prototype walkthroughs with our hostel wingmates and batchmates across 1st Year cycles and senior engineering branches.**
>
> **The peer feedback confirmed four major design breakthroughs:**
> - **1. Zero-Knowledge Safety:** Female students living in hostel blocks specifically praised that personal phone numbers and room numbers are never disclosed, completely eliminating unwanted calls or messages.
> - **2. On-Spot UPI Trust:** Peers loved inspecting the textbook or calculator physically before scanning the senior's direct GPay/PhonePe QR code, avoiding any risk of advance online payment scams.
> - **3. Relief from 'DM for Price':** Freshers confirmed huge relief from transparent fixed pricing, avoiding awkward price haggling on WhatsApp.
> - **4. Campus Landmark Meetings:** Predefined spots like the Central Library Ground Floor turnstiles made meeting peers completely natural and safe.
>
> **For Future Scope (Version 2.0), we plan to introduce:**
> 1. Automated WhatsApp Cloud API notifications for instant pings when an item is requested.
> 2. Smart campus locker integration at the Central Library for 24/7 contactless handovers.
> 3. Automatic calendar reminders 7 days before end-sems to return borrowed books.
>
> **In conclusion, this Design Thinking project proved to us that solving campus problems isn't about complex algorithms, but about deep human empathy. By recirculating resources, CampusShare saves students money and reduces paper and electronic waste.**
>
> **Thank you, ma'am, for your guidance. We now welcome questions and feedback!"**

## Video Recording Checklist

1. **Platform:** Open Zoom, MS Teams, or Google Meet.
2. **Webcams:** Ensure all team members have cameras switched ON and good lighting.
3. **Screen Share:** One member shares their screen (Full screen mode `F11` for presentation and browser).
4. **Audio:** Use earphones or headsets to ensure crisp voice audio without room echo.
5. **Length:** Keep within 5 to 7 minutes.
6. **File Format:** Save as `.mp4` and upload to your submission drive or YouTube (Unlisted).
