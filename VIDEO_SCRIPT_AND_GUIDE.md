### [0:00 – 2:45] Member 1: Introduction, Empathy & Problem Analysis

*(Share screen: PowerPoint Presentation in Slide Show mode `F11`)*  
*(Start on Slide 1, then advance through Slides 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8)*

> **[Slide 1: Title Slide]**  
> *"A very warm welcome to Dr. Vandana Jha ma'am and everyone evaluating our Design Thinking project. Today, my teammate [Member 2 Name] and I are presenting our project: **CampusShare**, a verified peer-to-peer campus resource and book exchange designed specifically for engineering students at MIT Bengaluru."*

> **[Slide 2 & 3: Context & The Campus Problem]**  
> *"Every semester, undergraduate engineering students are required to procure specialized academic supplies—such as B.S. Grewal's Higher Engineering Mathematics, Casio fx-991 scientific calculators, engineering drafters, and chemistry lab coats. Students incur heavy recurring out-of-pocket expenses purchasing brand-new supplies each term. The tragedy is that once end-semester examinations conclude, these expensive tools sit completely idle and neglected in hostel cupboards."*

> **[Slide 4 & 5: Phase 1 — Empathy & Field Research]**  
> *"During our Empathy phase, we conducted observational field research directly on active MIT Bengaluru student WhatsApp channels. Our research revealed that 88% of freshers spend between ₹2,500 and ₹4,500 every semester on gear used for only 16 weeks, while 92% of senior students have 3 or more textbooks and instruments locked in hostel rooms.*
>
> *Furthermore, students constantly try to trade surplus items—such as accidental duplicate Amazon orders like MacBook covers, midnight food surplus to prevent hostel waste, sealed pantry food, gym supplements, and even splitting digital study subscriptions! However, unstructured WhatsApp groups completely fail: notices get buried within minutes under hundreds of spam messages, sellers play frustrating 'DM for price' games, and female students are uncomfortable sharing personal contact numbers on public groups."*

> **[Slide 6 & 7: Phase 2 — Define & 5-Whys Root Cause]**  
> *"Based on our field research, our official problem statement is:*  
> **'Undergraduate engineering students at MIT Bengaluru incur substantial recurring financial burdens each semester purchasing temporary academic resources that are abandoned after single-term use, because informal campus communication channels lack verification, itemized search, and structured peer-to-peer lending models—leaving senior student inventory unutilized.'**
>
> *To diagnose the root cause, we performed the 5-Whys technique:*  
> *1. Why do freshers overspend on tools? Because they buy brand-new copies from outside commercial shops.*  
> *2. Why don't they get them from seniors? Because seniors leave them locked in hostel rooms.*  
> *3. Why don't seniors sell or lend them? Because posting on social groups is unorganized and tedious.*  
> *4. Why are social groups ineffective? Because chat apps lack categories, search filters, and upfront price tags.*  
> *5. Root Cause: There is no dedicated, campus-exclusive web portal tailored for student resource lending and physical handovers."*

> **[Slide 8: Phase 3 — Solve & Multi-Tier Exchange Model]**  
> *"This led us to our Solve Phase where we designed a multi-tier exchange system:*  
> *• **🚨 Express Exam & Lab SOS:** For emergency 1-day or 3-hour needs—like when a student forgets their calculator 30 minutes before an end-sem exam or needs a lab coat for an unexpected practical viva. They can rent it for just ₹20 right outside the exam hall.*  
> *• **Borrow for Free:** For semester-long textbooks.*  
> *• **Rent at Low Cost:** For 2-week exam blocks.*  
> *• **Buy at 75% Discount:** For permanent handover from graduating seniors.*  
> *• **⚡ Flash Drops & Subscriptions:** For zero food waste and safe digital subscription splits.*  
>
> *Now, I will hand over the presentation to my teammate, [Member 2 Name], who will demonstrate our live interactive working prototype and discuss our user validation results."*

### [2:45 – 5:45] Member 2: Live Prototype Walkthrough & Key Innovations

*(Member 2 shares screen: Browser showing `prototype/index.html` or live GitHub Pages demo: `https://capsid85.github.io/CampusShare/`)*

> **[Show Navbar & Verification Badge]**  
> *"Thank you, [Member 1 Name]. As you can see on the screen, this is our live working prototype for CampusShare.*  
>
> *Notice at the top right that platform access is restricted exclusively to verified `@learner.manipal.edu` student credentials. In the hero section, we display our three core design pillars: Zero Academic Waste, 100% Verified student accounts, and Zero Platform Intermediary Fees."*

> *(Click on '🚨 Exam & Lab SOS (1-Day / Single Use)' filter tab)*  
> *"Here is our most impactful innovation: **'Exam & Lab SOS'**. Most students don't need to own a calculator all year; they panic on the morning of a math exam when their battery dies, or when they forget their lab apron in the hostel. Instead of being forced to buy a new ₹1,500 calculator or miss their practical exam, a student can click '🚨 Instant SOS Claim' for a ₹20 slot and pick it up right outside the Exam Hall Gate or Science Lab within 5 minutes."*

> *(Click on '⚡ Flash Drops (Food & Hostel Surplus)' filter tab)*  
> *"Next, we solved the frustrating 'DM for price' spam and food waste on campus. Taking inspiration directly from real WhatsApp group posts, students can list 3:25 AM egg parathas or sealed pantry snacks with live countdown timers, or accidental duplicate MacBook cases from Amazon. Every listing has 100% upfront fixed pricing—completely eliminating awkward price haggling."*

> *(Click on '🎟️ Subscriptions & Digital Splits' filter tab)*  
> *"In our Digital Subscriptions section, verified students can safely share private profile slots on their personal Netflix or Spotify Family plans for ₹99 or ₹40 a month. Because accounts are tied to verified `@learner.manipal.edu` emails, advance payment defaults and credential theft are completely prevented."*

> *(Click on 'Borrow (Free)' filter tab)*  
> *"For full-semester coursework, freshers can filter for 100% Free Borrowing—for example, this copy of Higher Engineering Mathematics by B.S. Grewal listed by a 3rd Year CSE senior, with pickup right at the Central Library Ground Floor."*

> *(Point cursor to card specs: 'Handover Spot' and 'Ghost Privacy')*  
> *"A crucial breakthrough of our platform is the **Zero-Knowledge Ghost Privacy Architecture**: On the public feed, we NEVER display student phone numbers or hostel room numbers—only public campus landmarks like the Central Library or Food Court. This protects student safety and completely eliminates spam, especially for female students living in campus hostels."*

> *(Click 'Chat & Meet at Spot' on an item to open the slide-over drawer)*  
> *"When a student clicks 'Chat & Meet at Spot', an Apple-style slide-over chat drawer opens. Students can coordinate meeting times directly with verified peers using quick suggestion chips—without ever leaking their phone numbers."*

> *(Click '📍 Ping "I've Arrived at Spot"' button in drawer)*  
> *"When arriving at the campus landmark, the buyer clicks 'Ping Arrival Beacon' with a visual cue like 'wearing a black hoodie'. The seller gets an instant ping and steps out."*

> *(Click '⚡ On-Spot UPI QR' in drawer)*  
> *"Most importantly, there is NO platform escrow holding student pocket money. The buyer inspects the textbook or calculator physically first. Once satisfied, the seller displays their direct UPI QR code right inside the app. The buyer scans it with GPay or PhonePe for instant direct settlement, and both confirm the handover."*

> *(Click on '+ List an Item' button in navbar)*  
> *"Seniors can also click 'List an Item' to post an unused tool in under 30 seconds with automatic Zero-Knowledge Ghost Privacy enabled by default."*

### [5:45 – 6:45] Member 2: Testing Results, Future Roadmap & Conclusion

*(Member 2 switches screen share back to Slide 13 in PowerPoint)*  
*(Advance through Slides 13, 14, 15)*

> **[Slide 13 & 14: Phase 5 — Testing & User Validation]**  
> *"To validate our prototype, we conducted walkthrough sessions with hostel wingmates and batchmates across 1st-year common cycles and senior engineering branches. The peer feedback confirmed four major design breakthroughs:*  
> *1. **Zero-Knowledge Safety:** Female students living in hostel blocks specifically praised that personal phone numbers and room numbers are never disclosed.*  
> *2. **On-Spot UPI Trust:** Peers loved inspecting the physical item before scanning the direct GPay/PhonePe QR code, completely eliminating advance online payment scams.*  
> *3. **Relief from 'DM for Price':** Freshers confirmed huge relief from transparent fixed pricing.*  
> *4. **Campus Landmark Meetings:** Designated meetup spots like the Central Library turnstiles made peer meetings safe and natural."*

> **[Slide 15: Future Scope & Conclusion]**  
> *"For Future Scope (Version 2.0), we plan to introduce:*  
> *1. Automated WhatsApp Cloud API notifications for instant item pings.*  
> *2. Smart campus locker integration at the Central Library for 24/7 contactless handovers.*  
> *3. Automated calendar alerts 7 days before end-sems to return borrowed books.*  
>
> *In conclusion, this Design Thinking project proved to us that solving campus challenges is fundamentally about deep human empathy. By recirculating resources, CampusShare saves students money and reduces academic waste.*  
>
> *Thank you very much, Dr. Vandana Jha ma'am, for your continuous guidance throughout this semester. We now welcome any questions and feedback!"*
