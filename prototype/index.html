HUM_4342 Design Thinking

Innovation Methodology Architecture

Phase 1: Empathy

Field Observation

Audit of active campus WhatsApp trading groups revealed friction from 'DM for price' spam, rapid chat message decay, and lack of search.

Phase 2: Analyze

5-Whys Root Cause

Seniors hoarded resources due to informal friction. The root cause was the absence of a structured, campus-exclusive handover platform.

Phase 3: Solve

Multi-Tier Exchange

Tri-modal exchange architecture supporting Free Borrowing, Weekly Rentals, Flash Surplus Drops, and Verified Digital Subscription Splitting.

Phase 4: Prototype

Peer Walkthrough & Testing

User feedback confirmed that Zero-Knowledge privacy, arrival beacons, and direct on-spot UPI eliminate student safety and scam concerns.
